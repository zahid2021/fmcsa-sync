A) run.py mein route hai ya nahi?
---------------------------------
cd /d C:\Users\Administrator\Desktop\fmsca\fmsca_backend
findstr /C:"export/drive" run.py
findstr /C:"export/drive" backend.py
findstr /C:"export/drive" new_backend.py

Agar new_backend.py mein DIKHE lekin run.py mein NA DIKHE:
  copy /Y new_backend.py run.py
  copy /Y new_backend.py backend.py
  findstr /C:"export/drive" run.py

Agar new_backend.py mein BHI na dikhe → Notepad se pehle paste:

notepad C:\Users\Administrator\Desktop\fmsca\fmsca_backend\new_backend.py
  (local new_backend.py poora Ctrl+A Ctrl+C → Notepad Ctrl+A Ctrl+V → Ctrl+S)

phir:
copy /Y new_backend.py run.py
copy /Y new_backend.py backend.py

---------------------------------

Last column = PID. Us window ko Ctrl+C se band karo.
Agar pata na chale kaunsi window:
  tasklist | findstr python
